Hi, Jess

Welcome to our digital music diary!

Happy anniversary, I love and miss you so much!

Being with you has made the past year full of love, joy, fun, and lots and lots of good music.

You deserve the entire world, but I hope this long-awaited playlist is a good start...

This 65 song playlist includes 5 songs for each of the last 12 months.

I've tried (to the best of my abilitity) to use these songs to encapsulate the mood, changes, memories, and adventures we've had each month.

This includes songs we've enjoyed independently,showed each other, or listened to together.

I can't wait for you to listen, and to continue adding to it for the rest of our lives.
